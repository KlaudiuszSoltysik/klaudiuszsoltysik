# Hi there! 👋

Welcome to my personal GitHub space. 

By day, I work in tech, but this space is purely my personal playground. The projects you'll find here are extensions of my curiosity, hobbies, and random ideas that I wanted to test out or build for fun.

---

### 💡 What's inside?

Here you’ll find a mix of experiments, side projects, and proof-of-concepts spanning across:
- **IoT & Hardware Integration** — connecting sensors, telemetry pipelines, and edge devices.
- **Cloud & Kubernetes Playground** — self-hosted homelab setups, GitOps experiments, and custom schedules.
- **Geospatial & Mobile Apps** — mapping experiments and location-aware tools.
- **AI & ML on the Edge** — lightweight local LLM setups, custom schedulers, and automation scripts.
- ...and random scripts or mini-games whenever I get curious about a new framework or language.

---

### 📌 A Quick Disclaimer

Because I treat coding here strictly as a hobby:
* **Work in progress:** Some projects are polished, while others are half-baked ideas or abandoned mid-way once the main problem was solved.
* **No clear direction:** You won't find a single theme here — just whatever caught my interest at the moment.
* **Tinkering > Perfection:** Code quality varies depending on whether I was meticulously building something useful or quickly hacking together a midnight prototype.
